Getting Started With FR Legends

FR Legends is a drift-focused driving game where car selection, tuning, weight balance, steering control, and throttle management all affect how a car behaves. New players can make faster progress by learning the basic handling mechanics before spending too much time changing individual setup values.

If you are trying to understand the available cars and their differences, this FR Legends car guide is a useful starting point.

Learning the Game

A good setup cannot replace consistent driving technique. Players should first become comfortable with steering inputs, throttle control, transitions, and maintaining a predictable drift line. Once those fundamentals feel natural, tuning changes become much easier to understand.
